How to Use

Add an expense

Type the amount and what it was in the box at the bottom, e.g. 12.50 milk and bread, then tap send. Note, Camera and Gallery under the box add a note or a receipt photo. You can also share a receipt photo or a payment message from another app straight into House Expenses.

Hold an expense

Press and hold an expense (right-click on a computer) for Share, Add again, Edit and Delete.

Settle up

More → Settle Up shows what's open with each housemate across every cycle. Tap "Settled" when they've paid you, or "I've paid" when you've paid them.

Hashtag bills

Add a tag like #groceries to an expense to keep it in its own pot. Tap the tag on the balance card to see only that bill.

Who's in this month

In Settlements, tap "Active housemates". Untick someone who's away and the split works out without them.

Recovery Vault

Deleted something by accident? Settings → Recovery Vault brings it back until it expires.
