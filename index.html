How to Use

Add an expense

Type the amount and what it was in the box at the bottom, e.g. 12.50 milk and bread, then tap send. The ⋮ button adds a note or a receipt photo.

Swipe actions

Turn on "Swipe to reveal actions" in Settings, then swipe an expense sideways for Share, Edit and Delete.

Hashtag bills

Add a tag like #groceries to an expense to keep it in its own pot. Tap the tag on the balance card to see only that bill.

Who's in this month

In Settlements, tap "Active housemates". Untick someone who's away and the split works out without them.

Recovery Vault

Deleted something by accident? Settings → Recovery Vault brings it back until it expires.
